**Why not shipped:** provinces are Voronoi cells with seeds pushed apart (MIN=27 px), so Britain's 12 seeds drift into the Channel (Paris sits next to London, UK cells overlap France), US states are not visible as distinct shapes, garrisons/army counts per province dropped (London garrison 6). A real fix needs: (1) a local projection magnifier for Britain/Ireland, the US east and Australian east coasts (LX/LY in the generator), (2) real border polygons (e.g. Natural Earth admin-1, public domain) clipped to land instead of Voronoi, (3) per-province garrison/economy rebalance, (4) grand tests + saves (province indices change; bump the grand save version), (5) a look pass on the 3D map (borders, labels, flags).
Main is untouched by this; the earlier GB/AU/CA nation work on main is unaffected.
