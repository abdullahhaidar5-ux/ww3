# Grand strategy — design and stage plan

Abdullah 2026-11-05 19:16: "This entire game is just one layer. There will be a total war / EU4 style grand map view where a user
can control their empire high level, but they can choose to engage in battles and play tactically." His decisions are in
CLAUDE.md ("Grand strategy layer") and orch/GRAND-PICKS.md. This file is the design; stage 1 (grand1) shipped the foundation.

## Principles
- **Simultaneous turns.** Everyone plans, then all orders resolve together in a fixed order: AI plans → movement → contested
  provinces (battles) → capture → economy/upkeep/supply → elimination + victory check → next turn.
- **Deterministic.** Turn resolution is a pure function of (state, orders, seed): every random draw comes from `mulberry` seeded with
  `seed`, `turn` and a stable index; no `Math.random`, `Date` or map-iteration order. `grHash()` hashes the state; `orch/grandtest.mjs`
  runs 10 AI-only turns twice and compares. That is what lets friends multiplayer (stage 7) be added without a rewrite.
- **Solo vs AI empires first.** Every nation not played by you is an AI empire (`grAIPlan`).
- **World with detail where it matters.** 174 land provinces in the five focus regions (Middle East & N. Africa, Europe/Russia, East
  Asia, South Asia, North America & Caribbean) plus South America, Africa, SE Asia and Oceania, 44 sea zones (ocean lanes cost 2–3
  turns, straits are flagged chokepoints). Stylised; no real political claims. Generated by `orch/grandmap-gen.mjs` into
  `src/13-grandmap.js` (Voronoi cells around warped lon/lat seeds; adjacency from the cell raster; rivers listed).
- **Battles use the real RTS.** A "Fight it" battle is a Conquest battle whose ONLY forces are the armies' real units (see below).
- **One system per idea** (orch/SYSTEMS.md): empire economy is grand2's, generals/fleets grand3's, and so on.

## Files
`src/13-grand.js` engine (state, turn, AI, auto-resolve, economy, save) · `src/13-grandmap.js` map data (generated) ·

## Stage 1 (grand1) — what exists
- **Map screen** (canvas): pan (drag / WASD / arrows), wheel zoom, nine quick-jump region buttons, province tooltip, owner colours with
  terrain tint, towns, oil/farm/mine/port pips, capital star + war-goal ring, chokepoints as red diamonds, army chips with the nation colour and
  unit count, dotted march paths, turn log, End turn (Enter). Left-click selects, **right-click orders** the selected army.
- **Provinces**: owner, terrain, towns, resources, garrison (unit list), tax Low/Normal/High, unrest, conquered-recently flag, recruit queue,
  linked battle map (real places where one exists: Baghdad→Mesopotamia, Istanbul→Bosphorus, Kyiv→Dnipro, …, otherwise by terrain; river
  crossing → Twin Rivers; mountain pass → Khyber/Zagros).
- **Nations**: every nation that owns a province. Full nations (own `FACTIONS` entry) use their own units; others are minor nations with
  a shared regional army (`GR_PROXY`). Same start strength for every full nation (`GR_VBASE`), minors get half. The EU is not a nation.
  A nation that gets a `FACTIONS` entry later (Iraq, Syria, Japan, …) becomes full automatically.
- **Armies**: lists of unit records `[kind, health %, experience, ammo %]`; recruit in owned provinces (money, 1–2 turns), split / merge,
  move along links (land 1 turn, +1 over a major river or into mountains; sea zones only between ports; oceans 2–3 turns).
  Unsupplied armies (not in or next to own land) lose men every turn (more in desert/mountain/snow, a little at sea).
- **Wars**: opening wars are a ring per region (every full nation starts with the same number of rivals; yours always ≥ 1). Marching into
  a neutral nation's province asks you to declare war first; the AI declares on its weakest neighbour when it has none.
- **Battles**: when a nation arrives in a province with a hostile army or garrison → prompt **Fight it live / Auto-resolve / Withdraw**.
  - *Auto-resolve* — 4 exchange rounds on unit value (cost × health × veterancy × ammo) with defender bonuses: base 12%, terrain
    (mountain +30, jungle +12, urban +15, river plain +10, …), river crossing +20, mountain pass +10, home turf +10, capital +8.
    Losers keep survivors only if they can fall back; survivors are wounded and gain experience.
  - *Fight it live* — a Conquest-engine battle with **exactly** the units of both sides (types, counts, health, veterancy, ammo; the army
    general is the Hero Squad unit), the defender also gets the garrison and 1–3 forts (Defense Guns, a SAM site for a capital). **No free base,
    no $10,000, nothing can be built or bought** (production orders are ignored, `G.grand`). Idle armies in neighbouring provinces arrive
    as timed reinforcement waves (first after 2 min) from the map edge behind their side.
  - *Win condition* (no base to destroy): a side with no units left loses; at 15:00 the side with more surviving force (cost × health) wins,
    the defender on a tie; **Retreat** gives the enemy the field. Survivors, wounds and experience are written back into the same records,
    the dead are removed, losers fall back to their previous (or an owned adjacent) province or are destroyed.
- **Capture**: a province with no garrison and only enemy armies changes hands (+35 unrest, half income for 3 turns). Losing the capital
  moves it to the richest remaining province and is flagged "peace talks come in stage 4".
- **Economy (basic)**: income = towns×60 + oil 150 + farm 40 + mine 60 + port 40, ×1.25 capital, × tax (0.55 / 1 / 1.6), reduced by unrest;
  upkeep 0.35% of army value per turn. Unrest drifts to a target set by tax, foreign ownership and recent conquest; above 72% a province may revolt
  (garrison halves). Garrisons regrow. Empire economy proper is stage 2.
- **Victory**: hold 35% of the world's provinces, or every war-goal province of every other full nation in your region, or (Quick, 25 turns)
  best score at the end (provinces×10 + war goals×25 + army value/2000). **Defeat**: no provinces, or no capital and no armies.
- **Save**: the campaign is saved to `localStorage['ww3grand1']` at every turn end and after orders; Continue resumes it.
- **Menu**: Grand campaign · Conquest · Missions · Tutorial. The old story missions are called "Missions" everywhere players see it.

## Known limits of stage 1 (handed to later stages)
No fleets (armies sail as transports; hostile armies at sea do not fight), no allies, no peace deals (wars end when a nation is eliminated),
reinforcement waves apply to live battles only (auto-resolve uses just the armies present), nation names for unshipped full nations
are minor nations until their factions land, AI is simple (merge, march on the weakest reachable enemy province, recruit at the front).

## Stage 2 (grand2) — empire economy: what exists
Code: `src/13g-grandeco.js` (all rules, `ge*`), `src/13h-grandecoui.js` (Economy panel, province controls, icons). State: `GC.nat[c].e` (fuel, food, man, bank, bonds, tw, ration, sell, oil, mer, emb, ord, pw, dry, op, br = last turn's breakdown) and `GC.eco` (crisis schedule, closed straits, oil index); province fields `bl` (buildings `[kind,damage%]`, shared with the battle mirror), `bq` (queue), `au/aut/auC` (autonomy), `tr/cf/ctp` (city tier/focus), `sc/lt` (scorched/looted until turn). Turn order in `grFinishTurn`: capture → `geTurnStart` (power grid) → province loop (`geProvInc`: resources + buildings + city tier × autonomy × tax × crises) → `geAfterProvinces` (actions, then per nation: trade, tolls, merchants, sanctions/smuggling, oil → refinery → fuel, food, manpower, upkeep, bonds, bank, total-war/ration/starvation unrest; then buildings/cities finish, armies resupply, world oil price) . Orders (human UI and AI call the same functions): `geBuild geAutonomy geCity geBorrow geRepay geDeposit geWithdraw geTotalWar geRation geSellMode geEmbargo geMerchant geSetStrait geAct(bomb|loot|scorch)`.
- **Buildings** (slots 3 + dev/2 + city tier): base (recruit 1 turn sooner, +2 garrison, supply hub), airfield (sorties via `geSortieStock`, stands in the battle), port (+50, sea trade, shipyard), factory (+110, recruits −8% each up to 3, needs power), refinery (16 fuel/turn), power plant (+14 grid), air defence (SAM; halves bombing), irrigation (+6 food), deep mine (+100), fort, Mountain Fortress (IR, RS, AF, KD, DW, KP, mountain provinces; pre-built; arrives as a bunker in the defender's battle).
- **Economy**: tax ×  autonomy (Direct 1.0 / Local .82 / Autonomous .58 income; manpower 1/.7/.4; unrest 0/−9/−20); trade routes (neighbours 4.5/pt, sea lanes via ports, same-owner half), world trade −12% per closed strait, merchants (max 1–3, $70/turn, skim a province's trade), embargoes cut both ways, 3+ embargoers = sanctions (trade −50%, smugglers recover part, ×2 for CU/SD/AZ); oil index 55–190 swinging by turn + closed straits + crisis; fuel burnt by armies (tanks/arty/helis/AA 0.5, infantry 0.1, ×1.5 when marching) and airfields, shortfall imported if you can pay, dry stock → columns move every other turn, no sorties, battles start with low fuel; food (towns 2.8, farms +7, consumption 2.3/town + 0.12/unit; imports when short; starvation +8 unrest; rationing −20% use, +3 unrest, besieged hold); power grid ratio scales factories/refineries/mines and slows recruits; manpower pool (cap 60 + 14/town) gates recruiting (inf 7, other 4 per unit); upkeep 1.8% of army value × (1 + value/70000); war bonds $1500, +17% over 10 turns (+34% in a credit crunch, halved for AE/GR…), limit 2500 + 5×net, default → credit frozen 8 turns, unrest; central bank 1%/turn (2% with a bank asset), 50% lootable (20% with vault); total war (+30% manpower, −1 recruit turn, +15% upkeep, +3.5 unrest a turn; 4-turn cooldown); crises (market crash −15%, oil shortage, bad harvests, credit crunch, trade boom +10%) from a seeded schedule, same for all, halved with the crisis asset; salvage 10% of the value destroyed in a battle to the winner (×2 salvage asset); bombing ($250 + 8 fuel, airfield within range), loot (army present: 3× province income, +30 unrest, −20% income 5 turns), scorched earth ($150: works −60%, income halved, captor gets nothing) all feed `geOpinion` (world opinion hook for stage 4).
- **Signatures**: `orch/SIGNATURES.md` is generated from `GE_SIG`; E nations get two economy modifiers (each pair unique, ~equal value), F/M/L nations get the same-value interim stipend (tax ×1.05 + upkeep ×0.88) until grand3/grand6 ship their assets.
- **Battle inheritance**: armies keep `fu` (fuel %) and ammo % per unit; each turn they refill by supply level (hub / own land / next to own land / none); the battle's team fuel starts at the army's fuel, leftover returns. No battle money, no building in battles (grand1 rule).
- **Not yet** (handed on): the entry-edge supply trucks/convoys inside the battle; jets flying sorties (grand3 reads `geSortieStock`); opinion consequences (grand4); the flat 2D map has no building icons (3D map only).
