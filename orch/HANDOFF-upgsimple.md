# HANDOFF upgsimple — ready (branch orch/upgsimple, not pushed to main, no cigate)

## What changed
- **Upgrades follow buildings.** Each upgrade already had a research building (`UPG[k].at`); in the panel every upgrade button now has `data-tab="upg"` + `data-from="<building>"` (barracks, factory, airfield, hq, shipyard). Research is only possible while you own that building (button disabled, "Requires <building>"), one at a time per building with progress, the AI researches the same way (unchanged sim).
- **Count per building per nation:** Barracks 2 · War Factory 2 · Airfield 2 · Command Center 2 (+ Ground-Penetrating Radar for nations without tunnels = 3) · Shipyard 1. Max 4 per building (guard). Removed from Conquest/Battle: Smuggling Routes (it only counters Sanctions) and Hardened Firewall (counters Cyber/EMP) — both stay for the grand map. "Radwan Training" untouched (RS Barracks).
- **BUG FIXED on the way:** GB, DE, FR, RV, OB (and JP before its own table) copied their upgrades from a wrong key (`UPG['EG1']` instead of `eg1`), so they had NO upgrades (no building, no effect). They now get the Egypt-shaped set with their own names like every other nation.
- **Powers cut to 5-6 active per nation:** Recon, Paradrop, Barrage, Repair + the nation's own power (Shock and Awe, Shahed Swarm, ...) + Bunker Buster for US/IL/RU/CN only; Cash Bounty stays as the passive income perk. No longer offered in Conquest/Battle: Illumination Flares, Fund Militia, Call-In Reinforcements, Sanctions, Cyber Attack, EMP Strike (`POW_KEEP`/`powBan` in src/01-data.js; the grand map keeps them; old saves with those keys are ignored by the existing `POWERS[k]`/`sigBan` checks).
- **Doctrines kept as they are** (4 free picks + perks): they fit the cut list (Armor/Guerrilla/Air/Artillery). ORCH: say if you want the 20-perk tree folded away too — it is the biggest remaining clutter in the Powers tab.
- **Power marks:** every Build button of a power-using building has `data-pw="<n>"` (negative for Generator = gives) and `<span class="pw">⚡N</span>` (yellow = needs, green = gives); hover says "Needs power: N" / "Gives power: N"; while placing, a small label by the cursor shows "⚡ Power used / made" and turns red with "LOW POWER, powered buildings switch off" when the new building would take you over. Source of truth is the POWER table via `pwNeed(k)`.
- Not done / for ORCH: Strike Center (uplink) has no upgrades of its own today (strike upgrades would need new effects) — the contract `data-from="uplink"` works if you add some. The old Units/Upgrade panel is unchanged apart from the attributes (your bar src/08o-zhbar.js untouched).
