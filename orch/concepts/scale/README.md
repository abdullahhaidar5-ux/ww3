# Believable scale on the campaign map (helper "mapscale", 2026-11-11)

Before/after pictures of the same views (1600x900, Iran quick campaign, seed 99): Italy and Greece, Kuwait and Basra, Lebanon/Israel/Syria, north-east Iran, Tehran, Athens at the closest zoom, and the Middle East region view.
Before: one town and its beige plate covered a quarter of a country, houses were as big as provinces, soldiers stood in the sea and flags were taller than mountains.
After: every town is a small tight cluster inside its own province (ordinary town at most 1/6 of the province's width, capital or metropolis 1/4, no plate, none overlap), roads are thin lines, and army figures with their flags are no taller than their town is wide and stand on land beside it.
Armies on islands that the map does not draw at all (Crete, Sardinia, Hawaii and other small islands) now show as a ship instead of soldiers standing on water.
The region view and farther zooms are unchanged; in the middle zoom the town clusters are drawn 2.2 times bigger so they stay easy to find. Guard: "mapscale" in orch/grandtest.mjs.
