# HANDOFF roadcap (2026-11-15, helper)
Abdullah 2026-11-14 18:40 "Make it so that units can capture roads" (sections, any ground unit). Built in src/03c-logistics.js (lgRoadCap, lgRcOwner, lgRcFoe, lgCover sources), AI in lgAI2, ring in src/08c-logi.js, guard in orch/decisions.mjs.
- Section = one edge of the road graph. D.rc[edge]={o owner,c capturer,p progress}; ground units (any, not air/boats) within 55 of the road, no enemy ground unit on it: ~10 s, +50% per extra unit (max 6), contested = paused, progress fades. HQ/depot-covered roads count as theirs until taken.
- Held section = whole section is supply area (lgCover beads -> supplyPoints) and passes half a reach on past both ends; enemy-held section cuts your line (dashed). Colours reuse the existing green/red/gray (minimap always, ground with L). Ring + "Road captured"/"Road lost" log. State: D.rc in the lg pack (clients) and in the checkpoint (lgd).
- FINDING: Battle maps had NO road graph (town roadNet is stripped in Battle) although they carry hand-laid M.roads. lgInit now uses M.roads (x WK) as the graph when roadNet is empty. This also makes HQ/depot supply lines run along those roads in Battle. Maps without M.roads have no road sections.
